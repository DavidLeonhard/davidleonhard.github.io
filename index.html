I work with entrepreneurs on how new ventures can take on ecological and social challenges, and on the ecosystems, design knowledge and digital tools that help them grow.

About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Strategy and Entrepreneurship Department at ICN Business School and a member of CEREFIGE at the University of Lorraine. I co-lead the ICN Research Chair on Digital Transformation and Entrepreneurship.

At ICN, I head the Digital and Ecological Transformations module, in which more than 800 students work on sustainability challenges in AI-supported hackathons across Paris, Nancy and Berlin. On the Berlin campus, I founded the Master in Management specialisation in Innovation and Sustainability.

I received my PhD in Management from the University of Stuttgart. Before that, I studied Nonprofit Management and Public Governance (M.A., Berlin School of Economics and Law) and Art History and Economics (B.A., Friedrich Schiller University Jena).

Research

Sustainable entrepreneurship

How sustainability-driven ventures deal with the liabilities of being new and different, and how entrepreneurs contribute to sustainability transitions.

Entrepreneurial ecosystems

How ecosystems can preserve the social value orientation of founders, how places shape ventures, and what policy can do to put public value at the centre.

Design science and digital tools

Developing design knowledge with practitioners: frameworks and tools for digital transformation, generative AI in entrepreneurship education, and workshops that make research usable.

Projects and prototypes

Einstein Center for Early Disease Interception

Principal Investigator for education and entrepreneurship training in a Berlin-wide consortium of twelve institutions (total funding €6 million) developing new approaches to disease prevention. Since 2025.

Mauerpark Puls

A pro-bono prototype built with Freunde des Mauerparks e.V.: visitors scan a QR code in the park, leave a short text impression and a mood, and the anonymous responses appear live on a map. puls.mauerpark.info

Digital Value Hub

Digital maturity analysis of 63 Berlin SMEs and prototyping of digitalization projects, funded by the ERDF and the Berlin Senate; results disseminated through articles, an edited book, and events. HTW Berlin, 2016–2021.

Bauspiel.org

Co-founded a venture documenting and re-issuing a construction toy designed by Bauhaus artist Alma Siedhoff-Buscher, held in the collection of the Bauhaus-Archiv Berlin. 2015–2016.
